In short

  • Toos is a food-discovery app for Malaysia, operated by Toos The Place Store. Using it means you accept these terms; sharing a review, a place or a photo means you accept the community guidelines below. The app asks you to confirm this once, before your first post.
  • Share what you actually experienced, about real places, in your own words and pictures. Be honest, be decent, keep other people’s private details out of it.
  • Anything you share is public under your display name. You keep the rights to it; you give Toos permission to show it in the app and the moderators permission to remove it if it breaks the rules.
  • You can report any review, listing or reply from inside the app, and hide everything from a member you would rather not see. Reports go to a person, not a filter.
  • Content that breaks the rules is removed. Accounts that keep breaking them lose the ability to post, or are closed.

01Your account

You need an account (an email address and a password) to save places, write reviews, add places, suggest edits or claim a shop. You must be at least 13 years old. One person, one account; keep your password to yourself; what is posted from your account is yours to answer for. You can delete your account, and everything tied to it, from Settings › Delete account in the app or via this page. How we handle your data is in the privacy policy.

02What you share

Reviews, ratings, dish names, photos, places you add, edits you suggest, replies you write as a shop owner and, if you turn it on, your saved places are all public, shown with your display name and contributor badge. You own what you share. By sharing it you give Toos The Place Store a worldwide, royalty-free licence to store it, show it in the app and on toostheplace.com, and let other members see and interact with it, for as long as it stays on Toos. Delete it and the licence ends, except for copies already made by other people’s devices in the normal course of using the app.

Only share what you have the right to share: your own words and your own photos, or ones you have permission to use. A photo of a dish or a shopfront is fine; a photo lifted from a restaurant’s website or someone else’s review is not.

03Community guidelines

Toos is useful because people tell the truth about places they have actually been. Everything below follows from that.

Share

  • Reviews of places you have visited, about the food, the service and the room — things another diner can check for themselves.
  • Real places that serve food or drink, with their real name and location. A stall in a food court counts; a kitchen in your home does not.
  • Your own photos of what you ordered and where you sat.
  • Disagreement. A two-star review next to a five-star one is the point, not a problem.

Don’t share

  • Reviews you were paid or asked to write, reviews of your own shop or a competitor’s, or reviews of places you have not been to.
  • Harassment, threats, slurs or hate aimed at anyone — a reviewer, an owner, staff, a group of people. Criticise the chicken rice, not the person.
  • Private information: phone numbers, home addresses, car plates, faces of other customers or staff who did not agree to be in your photo.
  • Spam: adverts, links to other services, the same text posted on many places, made-up dish names, listings that are not food places.
  • Sexual content, graphic violence, or anything illegal in Malaysia, including defamatory claims you cannot back up.
  • Impersonation: pretending to be a shop, its owner, or another member.

04Reporting and hiding

Every review, listing and owner reply in the app has a Report action. Pick a reason, add a note if it helps, and it reaches a moderator — a person — the same day. You will see “Reported” on the item so you know it went through; the person you reported is not told who reported them.

If you would rather not see a particular member at all, choose Hide this member on any of their reviews. Their reviews and replies disappear from your view on every phone you sign in on, and you can undo it from Settings. Hiding is private; it does not report anyone.

05What we do about it

  • A moderator reads every report and either removes the content or dismisses the report. Removed reviews and replies are deleted; removed listings are marked closed so nothing links to them.
  • Content can be removed without a report when a moderator sees it breaks these rules.
  • A member who keeps breaking the rules loses the ability to post, and in serious cases the account is closed. Illegal content may be referred to the authorities.
  • If something of yours was removed and you think that was wrong, write to hitooscoffee@gmail.com with the place name and the date; a person will look again.

06Shop owners

Claiming a shop means proving you run it — a business document and a photo taken at the shop. A verified owner can correct the listing’s details, add hours and photos, and reply to reviews. Replies follow the same guidelines as reviews: answer the point, don’t attack the person, and don’t post anyone’s private details. Owners cannot remove or edit other people’s reviews; if one breaks the rules, report it like anyone else.

07What Toos is, and isn’t

Toos is a directory and a place for opinions. Reviews are the views of the people who wrote them, not ours, and we do not check that every claim in them is true. Opening hours, prices and addresses come from members, from shop owners and from OpenStreetMap, and can be out of date — check before you travel. The app is provided as it is; we fix problems as we find them but cannot promise it will always be available or error-free. To the extent Malaysian law allows, Toos The Place Store is not liable for loss arising from your use of the app or from content other people posted on it. These terms are governed by the laws of Malaysia.

08Changes

If the rules change in a way that matters, this page changes first and the app asks you to accept the new version before your next post. The app links to this page from Settings › About.
