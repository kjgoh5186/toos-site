Points and titles

Everything you add earns points, and your points earn a title shown next to your name.

Earning points

  • +10for sharing a place
  • +15the first time someone else saves or reviews it
  • +5for each review
  • +3more when the review has a photo

Up to 40 points a day.

Owner

Run the shop?

Shop owners can claim their listing. Send a photo of a business document and a photo of the shopfront, taken in the app at the shop.

Once the claim is approved, you keep the opening hours, menu, photos and tags right, and reply to reviews as the owner.

The proof photos are kept private and deleted as soon as the claim is approved or rejected.
